Seed Search

Find seeds where a chosen starting dish + heat level offers specific cards on specific days. Runs entirely on the server, as a cancellable background job you can watch progress on - not in your browser, so it won't struggle on a low-powered device.

Sub-option
Day targets
What does this search for?

Restricts to seeds where days 1, 2, and 4 offer Research Desks.

What does this assume?

Assumes you can discard the offered pair once per night (the Bay Leaves plant) and take the alternate roll if it lands a wanted card. A seed can match because a target is only reachable that way.

Advanced options
Franchise carryover cards (already owned from a prior franchise run)
