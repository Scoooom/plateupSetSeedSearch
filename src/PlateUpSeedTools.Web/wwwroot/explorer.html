Seed Explorer

Walk a seed day-by-day (from the prep day before day 1, onward - including overtime days past day 15, shown with a caveat since those aren't ground-truth-verified) and see exactly what cards it offers. Pick a card to advance, or go back to an earlier day to see what the other option would have led to - both branches stay in the tree below, nothing is thrown away. No blueprint or customer-count data here, just card offers.
